GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Do you charge a call-out fee for quotes?

No — assessments and quotes are free. You only pay once you've agreed to the price and the work goes ahead.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Plettenberg Bay and cover the wider Bitou Municipality and Garden Route, including Knysna, Keurboomstrand, Natures Valley and The Crags.

Can you issue an electrical Certificate of Compliance (CoC)?

Yes. We issue CoC certificates for property sales, insurance requirements, and general compliance checks after inspecting and, where needed, correcting the installation.

Do you handle emergency call-outs?

Yes — burst pipes, total power loss and similar emergencies get priority. Call or WhatsApp us directly and we'll advise on the fastest way to get to you.

Do you work with holiday home and Airbnb owners while they're away?

Regularly. We handle everything from a single reactive repair to ongoing maintenance plans, and we're comfortable coordinating directly with guests or a property manager on your behalf.
